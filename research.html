Research

My research focuses on computational methods for partial differential equations, with emphasis on stochastic modelling, data assimilation, and scientific computing.

I work on numerical methods for convection–diffusion and reaction–diffusion systems, as well as stochastic PDEs arising in fluid dynamics and uncertainty quantification.

Currently, I develop particle filtering and nudging-based data assimilation methods for high-dimensional stochastic systems, with applications to fluid models and numerical weather prediction.
