Research

I develop computational methods for deterministic and stochastic PDEs and combine them with probabilistic machine learning. The main application is geophysical fluid dynamics.

Click a segment for details and papers.

Structure-Preserving Numerics

Compatible finite element discretisations that conserve energy and respect the geometric structure of geophysical fluid models, in both deterministic and stochastic settings.

  • Energy-conserving compatible space–time FEM for deterministic and stochastic rotating shallow water — under review, J. Comput. Phys. (arXiv:2610.13624)
  • A comparative numerical study of stochastic Hamiltonian Camassa–Holm equations — Proc. R. Soc. A (2026)
  • A multiscale Camassa–Holm equation — Chaos, Solitons & Fractals (2026)

Bayesian Data Assimilation

Particle filters with nudging for high-dimensional fluid models, including the global Girsanov nudged particle filter, which uses a Girsanov change of measure to steer particles towards observations.

  • Data assimilation using a global Girsanov nudged particle filter — Statistics and Computing (2026)
  • Data assimilation for the stochastic Camassa–Holm equation using particle filtering — Mathematics of Planet Earth (2024)
  • The GONG particle filter framework for data assimilation — CASML 2024

Generative Modelling

Score-based diffusion and flow-matching models as priors and samplers for Bayesian data assimilation.

Physics-Informed ML

Consistent CutPINNs: physics-informed neural networks that solve PDEs on curved level-set domains without body-fitted meshes.

  • Consistent CutPINNs for elliptic PDEs on curved level-set domains — under review (arXiv:2605.25562)
  • Consistent CutPINNs for convection–diffusion equations on curved level-set domains — under review (arXiv:2606.29147)

Numerical Analysis

Robust, convergent finite element and finite difference methods for PDEs on moving domains, singularly perturbed multiscale systems, and the radiative transfer equation.

  • An implicitly extended Crank–Nicolson scheme for the heat equation on a time-dependent domain — J. Sci. Comput. (2024)
  • Operator-splitting FEM for the radiative transfer equation — Numerical Algorithms (2025)
  • A discrete-ordinate weak Galerkin method for the radiative transfer equation — Appl. Numer. Math. (2024)
  • Parameter-uniform methods for singularly perturbed systems — 2018–2021 (see publications)
