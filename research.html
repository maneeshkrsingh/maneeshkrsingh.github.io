Research

My research focuses on the development of computational methods for partial differential equations (PDEs), with an emphasis on stochastic modelling, data assimilation, and scientific machine learning. I work at the intersection of numerical analysis and applied computational science, aiming to design scalable and structure-preserving algorithms for complex dynamical systems.

Core Themes

  • Data assimilation and Bayesian filtering
  • Stochastic Modelling and uncertainty quantification
  • Scientific machine learning for physical systems
  • Numerical methods for PDEs on fixed and moving domains

Current Focus

My current work involves developing advanced Bayesian particle filtering methods for stochastic PDEs, including nudging-based and probabilistic approaches. These methods are designed to integrate observational data into high-dimensional dynamical systems, with applications in fluid dynamics and numerical weather prediction.

Applications

  • Geophysical fluid dynamics
  • Numerical weather prediction
  • Multiscale and stochastic systems
  • Data-driven scientific computing
