Research

My research integrates stochastic modelling, numerical analysis, and scientific machine learning towards a unified framework for Bayesian scientific computing. The goal is to develop mathematically rigorous and computationally efficient methods for uncertainty quantification in complex dynamical systems.

Current Focus

My current work develops advanced Bayesian particle filtering methods for stochastic PDEs, including nudging-based and probabilistic approaches. These methods are designed to integrate observational data into high-dimensional dynamical systems, with applications in geophysical fluid dynamics and numerical weather prediction.

Applications

  • Geophysical fluid dynamics
  • Numerical weather prediction
  • Multiscale and stochastic systems
  • Data-driven scientific computing
