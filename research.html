Research

My research sits at the interface of scientific computing and probabilistic machine learning. On the computing side, I build stochastic models of physical systems and discretise them in ways that respect their conservation laws and geometric structure, backed by rigorous numerical analysis. On the probabilistic side, I combine these models with observations through Bayesian data assimilation and, more recently, generative models such as diffusion and flow matching. The main application is geophysical fluid dynamics.

Applications: geophysical fluid dynamics, numerical weather prediction, and multiscale stochastic systems. Click a segment for details and papers.

Stochastic Models and Structure-Preserving Numerics

Stochastic models of geophysical flows, and discretisations that respect their conservation laws and geometric structure, so that long simulations and stochastic ensembles stay physically meaningful.

  • Energy-conserving compatible space–time FEM for deterministic and stochastic rotating shallow water — under review, J. Comput. Phys. (arXiv:2610.13624)
  • A comparative numerical study of stochastic Hamiltonian Camassa–Holm equations — Proc. R. Soc. A (2026)
  • A multiscale Camassa–Holm equation — Chaos, Solitons & Fractals (2026)

Bayesian Data Assimilation for Stochastic PDEs

Sequential Bayesian filtering for high-dimensional stochastic PDEs. I develop advanced particle filters with nudging, including the global Girsanov nudged particle filter, which uses a Girsanov change of measure to steer particles towards observations. Test models include the stochastic Camassa–Holm and Euler equations, implemented in Firedrake.

  • Data assimilation using a global Girsanov nudged particle filter — Statistics and Computing (2026)
  • Data assimilation for the stochastic Camassa–Holm equation using particle filtering — Mathematics of Planet Earth (2024)
  • The GONG particle filter framework for data assimilation in SPDEs — CASML 2024

Generative Modelling for Data Assimilation

I develop score-based diffusion and flow-matching generative models as priors and samplers for Bayesian data assimilation in high-dimensional stochastic systems, building on the particle-filtering and nudging work above.

Physics-Informed Machine Learning

Physics-informed neural networks with provable consistency on complex geometries: consistent CutPINNs solve PDEs on curved level-set domains without body-fitted meshes.

  • Consistent CutPINNs for elliptic PDEs on curved level-set domains — under review (arXiv:2605.25562)
  • Consistent CutPINNs for convection–diffusion equations on curved level-set domains — under review (arXiv:2606.29147)

Numerical Analysis of PDEs

Robust and convergent finite element and finite difference methods: PDEs on time-dependent domains, singularly perturbed multiscale systems, and the radiative transfer equation.

  • An implicitly extended Crank–Nicolson scheme for the heat equation on a time-dependent domain — J. Sci. Comput. (2024)
  • Operator-splitting FEM for the radiative transfer equation — Numerical Algorithms (2025)
  • A discrete-ordinate weak Galerkin method for the radiative transfer equation — Appl. Numer. Math. (2024)
  • Parameter-uniform methods for singularly perturbed systems — PhD work, 2018–2021 (see publications)
