I work on computational methods for partial differential equations and data assimilation, with applications in geophysical fluid dynamics (GFD) and uncertainty quantification. My research also explores scientific machine learning and generative modelling for high-dimensional stochastic systems.

Currently Research Assistant Professor at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai. Previously at Imperial College London, University of Konstanz, and IISc Bangalore.

Research Highlights

Bayesian Data Assimilation for SPDEs: Particle filtering and nudging methods for high-dimensional stochastic GFD models.

Scientific Machine Learning: Ensemble score and flow-based generative models for data assimilation in complex dynamical systems.

Structure-Preserving Numerical Methods: Compatible FEM for deterministic and stochastic geophysical fluid models.

Numerical Analysis of PDEs: Eulerian and space-time FEM for PDEs on moving domains.
