I work on computational methods for partial differential equations and data assimilation, with applications in fluid dynamics and uncertainty quantification.

Currently Research Assistant Professor at SRM Institute of Science and Technology. Previously at Imperial College London, University of Konstanz, and IISc Bangalore.

Research Highlights

Stochastic Camassa–Holm Models: Structure-preserving stochastic transport models for fluid dynamics.

Data Assimilation for SPDEs: Particle filtering and nudging methods for high-dimensional stochastic systems.

Numerical Methods: Finite element and finite difference schemes for singularly perturbed PDEs.
