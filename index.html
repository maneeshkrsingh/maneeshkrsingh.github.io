I develop scalable computational methods for stochastic and deterministic partial differential equations, with applications in fluid dynamics, uncertainty quantification, and data assimilation. My work combines structure-preserving numerical methods with modern probabilistic and machine learning approaches.

I am currently a Research Assistant Professor at SRM Institute of Science and Technology, and previously held postdoctoral positions at Imperial College London, University of Konstanz, and IISc Bangalore.

Focus Areas

• Numerical Methods for PDEs (FDM, FEM)
• Data Assimilation & Particle Filtering
• Scientific Machine Learning
• Stochastic & Multiscale Modelling
