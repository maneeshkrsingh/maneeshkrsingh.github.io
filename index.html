Research Assistant Professor
Department of Mathematics
SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai

I work at the interface of scientific computing and probabilistic machine learning: Bayesian data assimilation for stochastic PDEs, structure-preserving finite element methods for geophysical fluids, physics-informed neural networks, and diffusion and flow-matching generative models.

Previously postdoctoral researcher at Imperial College London (2022–25), University of Konstanz (2021–22) and IISc Bangalore (2019–21). PhD in Mathematics, IIT Guwahati.

Research

Bayesian Data Assimilation Nudged particle filters for high-dimensional stochastic PDEs, including the global Girsanov nudged particle filter. Structure-Preserving Numerics Compatible, energy-conserving space–time FEM for deterministic and stochastic geophysical flows. Physics-Informed ML Consistent CutPINNs for PDEs on curved level-set domains, without body-fitted meshes. Generative Modelling Diffusion and flow-matching models as priors and samplers for data assimilation. Numerical Analysis of PDEs Robust, convergent finite element and finite difference methods: PDEs on moving domains, singularly perturbed multiscale systems, and radiative transfer.
