Teaching Approach

My teaching emphasizes conceptual clarity, mathematical rigor, and computational intuition. I aim to bridge theoretical analysis with modern computational tools, preparing students to work across applied mathematics, scientific computing, and data-driven modelling. Courses are designed to encourage problem-solving, independent thinking, and connections to real-world systems.
