Approach

My teaching emphasises conceptual clarity, mathematical rigour and computational intuition. I aim to bridge theoretical analysis with modern computational tools, preparing students to work across applied mathematics, scientific computing and data-driven modelling, with an emphasis on problem-solving, independent thinking and connections to real-world systems.
